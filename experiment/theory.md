**XOR AND XNOR GATES: DEFINITIONS, SCHEMATICS, AND TRUTH TABLES**

**XOR (exclusive OR)**

For a 2-input XOR, the output is low when both inputs are the same (either both low or both high). The output is high if one and only one of the inputs is high. The function is addition modulo 2 and hence the gate is used in half adder circuits.

| 1 | 1 | 0 |

**XNOR (exclusive NOR)**

For a 2-input XNOR, the output is high when both inputs are the same (either both low or both high). The output is low if one and only one of the inputs is high.

| 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 | 0 | 0 |
